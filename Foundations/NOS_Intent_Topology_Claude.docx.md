## **SECTION 5 — THE FORMAL AXIOLOGY CONNECTION: WHY** **THIS MATTERS TO SCIENCE**

Here is the academic domain where I believe the intent topology has the most original

work has stalled and the NOS approach may genuinely offer something new.

#### **5A. What Formal Axiology Has Tried and Failed**


Formal axiology is the discipline (founded primarily by Robert S. Hartman in the 1950s-70s) that
attempts to treat value scientifically. Hartman's foundational work 'The Structure of Value' (1967)
proposed that goodness could be measured by how fully a thing fulfills its concept — a
quasi-mathematical approach.


The field has not broken through into mainstream science for one specific reason: Hartman
used a linear, cardinality-based approach. He assigned values based on how many properties a
thing instantiates. More properties = more good. This treats value as additive and unbounded.


The NOS intent topology does something fundamentally different: it treats value as cyclic and
resolution-seeking. A moral state is not evaluated by how many properties it has, but by where it
is in the loop cycle and whether it has a functioning activator. This is a categorically different
structure.

#### **5B. The NOS Contribution: Value as Cyclic Resolution**


The specific thing the NOS offers that Hartman could not is the concept of moral asymptote: the
state of .999999 as permanently close to resolution without achieving it. Hartman had no
equivalent. His system could not formally describe the person who appears to have all virtues
but lacks the activating intent — who is at .999999 but does not love.


The NOS can describe this precisely. And it can predict what happens: the loop remains at
.999999 indefinitely, accumulating STOR residue (the weight of unresolved near-truths), until
either the activator arrives from outside (mercy) or the loop begins to decay toward .142857
(drift back to neutral).


This is a formal description of what moral philosophers call 'the sin of omission' — not the doing
of wrong, but the persistent failure to supply the activating love that the loop is reaching for. The
NOS encodes it precisely.

#### **5C. The Testable Prediction from the Intent Topology**


If the intent topology is correct, it predicts specific patterns in human relational behavior that can
be measured. Here is the most concrete one:


**Prediction:** Relationships that operate at the 9-sum complement level (where the
participants carry complementary virtue registers) should show measurably higher rates
of reciprocal activation (each partner's loop-resolution rate increasing in the presence of
the other) than relationships where participants share the same virtue register position.


This is measurable. Attachment research (Bowlby, Ainsworth), close relationships research
(Gottman), and team effectiveness research (Edmondson's psychological safety work) all have
datasets that could be analyzed for this complementary-activation pattern.


It would require operationalizing loop positions as measurable behavioral/emotional variables —
which is difficult but not impossible. The virtue register could be approximated by existing
psychometric instruments (character strengths surveys, attachment style measures, etc.).
